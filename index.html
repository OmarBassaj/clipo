Features

Small app. Everything you need.

Clipo does one job — remembering what you copy — and does it carefully.

Saves every kind of copy

Plain and formatted text, links, images and files. Copy the same thing twice and it just moves to the top.

Instant search

Type a few letters to find anything. It ignores case and accents and understands ı, İ, ş, ğ, ü, ö and ç.

Folders & pins

Keep replies, addresses and snippets in your own folders — drag items in. Pinned and foldered items are never deleted.

Secure folders with a PIN

Passwords and private notes are encrypted on disk and lock automatically. Pasted secrets are wiped from the clipboard after 30 seconds.

Image preview

Press Space on any copied image to see it full size before you paste it.

Privacy controls

Pause recording, ignore apps like your password manager, skip card numbers and IBANs, and auto-delete old items.

Privacy

What you copy is nobody else's business.

Clipo never connects to the internet. There is no account, no cloud sync and no analytics — your history lives in one file on your PC.

No network access at all

Not for updates, not for crash reports, not for anything. Nothing leaves your computer.

Encrypted secure folders

Items in secure folders are encrypted with AES-256 and your PIN, protected by your Windows account. Opening the database file directly shows nothing readable.

Sensitive data stays out

Card numbers and IBANs aren't saved unless you say so, and apps you choose are ignored completely.

You decide how long

Keep 50 to 5,000 items, auto-delete after a few days, or pause recording with one click from the tray.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Clipo send my data anywhere?

No. Clipo has no network access at all — no sync, no updates, no telemetry. Everything stays in a database file on your PC.

Where is my history stored?

In %LOCALAPPDATA%\Clipo\clipo.db. Settings → Privacy can show you the file.

Can I change the Ctrl+Shift+V shortcut?

Yes. Open Settings → General, click the shortcut and press the keys you want.

What happens if I forget my PIN?

Items in secure folders can't be recovered without the PIN — that's what keeps them safe. Your normal history isn't affected.

Will it slow my PC down?

No. Clipo waits quietly in the tray using about 40 MB of memory and only wakes up when you copy something or press the shortcut.

How do I uninstall it?

Go to Windows Settings → Apps and uninstall Clipo. It asks whether to delete your saved history too, so you can keep it if you plan to come back.
